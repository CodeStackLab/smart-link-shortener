📅 Effective Date: 12 October 2026 • ⚖️ PECA 2016 & NCCIA Aligned • 🌐 Jurisdiction: Pakistan
🛡️

Qanooni Wazahat (Legal Notice)

Link64.online Pakistan ke Cyber Crime qawaneen (Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 — PECA) aur mutaliqa regulatory idaron (NCCIA, PTA, LEAs) ke qawaneen ki mukammal pabandi karta hai. Hamari service par fraud, phishing, ya ghair-qanooni activity karne wale kisi shakhs ko anonymity (ghumnaami) faraham nahi ki jati.

1️⃣

Ta'aruf Aur Daira-e-Kar (Introduction & Scope)

Link64.online aik smart URL shortening aur redirection platform hai. Yeh Privacy Policy wazeh karti hai ke jab aap hamari website par aate hain, account banate hain, short link create karte hain, ya kisi short link par click karte hain, to hum konsi maloomat collect, mehfooz (retain) aur istemal karte hain.

Hamari service istemal karne ka matlab hai ke aap is Privacy Policy mein darj sharaait aur tareeqay-kar se ittefaq karte hain. Agar aap ittefaq nahi karte to barah-e-karam service istemal na karein.

2️⃣

Maloomat Jo Hum Collect Karte Hain (Information We Collect)

Hum service ki behtari, fraud prevention aur qanooni taqazon ke mutabiq darj zail maloomat collect karte hain:

  • Account Registration Data: User ka chuna hua username, password ka cryptographically hashed version (bcrypt), role, aur account creation timestamp.
  • Link Creation Data: Short kiya gaya original destination URL, custom parameters, routing rules, aur link banane ka waqt.
  • Technical Traffic Logs (Visitor Clicks): Jab koi visitor kisi Link64 link par click karta hai, to system security aur analytics ke liye visitor ka IP Address, Browser User-Agent header, Operating System, Device Type (Mobile/Desktop), Referring Website (Referrer URL), aur Click ka exact timestamp record karta hai.
  • Abuse Reports & Support Communications: Abuse form ya official WhatsApp ke zariye report karne wale ka contact info aur reported link ki tafseelat.

⚠️ Note: Hum kisi bhi user ka payment card PIN, bank passwords, ya sensitive private biometric data collect nahi karte.

3️⃣

Data Collect Karne Ka Maqsad (Purpose of Data Processing)

Hum collect shuda maloomat ko darj zail maqasid ke liye istemal karte hain:

  • Redirection & Smart Routing: Visitor ko uske device ke mutabiq durust destination URL tak swiftly redirect karna.
  • Anti-Abuse & Cyber Security: Spam bots, brute force attacks, phishing, automated scrapers, aur DDoS hamlon ko identify aur block karna.
  • Abuse Investigations: Fraud ya illegal content ki shikayat par link aur uske banany walay technical footprints ki jaanch karna.
  • Analytics: Link owners ko click counts, platform statistics aur countries ki majmooi aggregate reports faraham karna.
  • Legal Compliance: Pakistan ke nafiz-ul-waqt qawaneen aur NCCIA / PTA ke taqazon ko poora karna.
4️⃣

Data Retention Aur Preservation (Log Storage Duration)

System ki technical capacity aur security compliance ke mutabiq data darj zail muddat ke liye mehfooz kiya jata hai:

  • Standard Traffic Logs: Aam redirection logs mamool ke mutabiq 30 se 90 din tak retain kiye ja sakte hain, jiske baad purana data rotate ya purge ho sakta hai.
  • Mashkook / Reported Links: Agar kisi link ke khilaf abuse report milti hai ya cyber security audit ke tehat investigation shuru hoti hai, to mutaliqa link, destination URL aur click logs ko qanooni aur investigation zaroorat ke tehat extended muddat ke liye preserve kiya ja sakta hai.
  • Registered User Accounts: User account details jab tak account active rahe mehfooz rehti hain. Violation par terminate kiye gaye accounts ka audit trail audit compliance ke liye retain kiya jata hai.
5️⃣

🏛️ Government, Law Enforcement & NCCIA Compliance

Link64.online Pakistan ke qawaneen, bil-khasoos Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016) ke mutabiq competent law enforcement agencies (jaise NCCIA — National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency, FIA Cyber Crime Wing, PTA, aur Adalaton) ke sath qanooni framework ke tehat ta'awun karta hai.

⚖️ Qanooni Disclosure: Agar kisi ba-ikhtiyar qanooni idaray (Law Enforcement Agency) ki taraf se kisi mashkook link, scam, ya cyber crime se mutaliq valid qanooni notice, warrant ya investigation requirement mosool hoti hai, to hum apne paas mojood technical records (IP addresses, destination URLs, timestamps, registration details) qanoon ke mutabiq faraham karne ke paband hain.

Hum kisi aisi ghair-qanooni activity ki himayat nahi karte jisse Pakistan ki riyasat, awaam ya kisi fard ko mali ya jismooni nuqsan pohnchaya jaye.

6️⃣

🚫 Zero Tolerance Policy (Mana Shuda Content)

Darj zail activities par Link64.online par mukammal pabandi hai. Is tarah ke kisi bhi amal par baghair kisi notice ke foran link block aur account terminate kiya jata hai:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Nabalighon ke jinsi istehsal par zero tolerance hai. Records foran block aur mutaliqa cyber crime authorities ko report kiye jate hain.
  • Financial Fraud & Scams: Easypaisa, JazzCash, Pakistani banks, ya kisi bhi mali idaray ke fake lottery, fake prize ya payment scams.
  • Phishing & Credential Theft: Facebook, Instagram, Google, TikTok ya doosray platforms ke jaali login pages.
  • Malware & Viruses: Nuqsandeh APK files, trojans, ransomware ya spyware distribute karna.
  • Terrorism & Extremism: Dehshat-gardi ya nafrat angaiz mawad.

Cookies Aur Local Storage (Cookies Usage)

Link64.online service chalane ke liye mehdood zaroori cookies aur browser local storage ka istemal karta hai:

  • Security Tokens: CSRF aur rate limiting ke maqasid ke liye istemal hoti hain.

Hum aapka personal data kisi third-party ad network ko marketing ya bechne (sell) ke liye share nahi karte.

Data Security (Data Ki Hifazat)

Hum data ki hifazat ke liye industry-standard security measures ikhtiyar karte hain:

  • Passwords ko standard hashing algorithms (bcrypt with salt) ke zariye encrypt rakha jata hai.
  • Automated IP firewall aur rate-limiting ke zariye unauthorized system access ko roka jata hai.
